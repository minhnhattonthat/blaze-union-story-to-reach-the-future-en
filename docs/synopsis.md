# Synopsis (for translators: don't give a reveal away early)

Prequel to Yggdra Union, set in the Bronquia Empire. Sources: English Wikipedia and fan wikis for the order and who's who; the script decides the wording.

**Chapters 1-3 (common).** Garlot, 17, from the slums of Tiera, leads the Blaze Justice Corps (ブレイズ義賊団) with childhood friends Siskier (scout, cheerful, secretly loves him) and Jenon (tactician, flirt, secretly loves Siskier). They hunt corrupt merchants like Norn, meet the child Eimi, and clash with the mercenary David. Medoute, a travelling woman in a tengallon hat, criticises their recklessness, then joins as mentor. Margrave Velleman takes the band into his service and renames it Gram Blaze. They rescue twin noble girls Aegina and Luciana from the Blessed Papal Army (assassins). Others who join: Byff (beast-man), the ghost Pamela, Cerica, Eater, Sleip, Lapis. Nessiah, a blind prophet and necromancer in the Kred forest, joins as strategist after they beat him. Chapter 4 reveals Eimi searches for a brother "Gulcasa".

**Routes.** The game branches at a choice:
- **A (Garlot / Gulcasa, the true route).** Siskier dies. Garlot learns "Garlot" is a seal his mother put on him; he is Gulcasa, pure-blood heir of the demon god Brongaa. He unseals his blood, overthrows Emperor Soltier and becomes Emperor, leading into Yggdra Union. Allies desert as he changes. Nessiah grows close to him while nudging him toward war. Eimi reveals she is his half-sister Emilia.
- **B (Aegina).** Luciana is killed. Aegina, a Fantasinian princess cast out because twins are unlucky, attacks Fantasinia with Gram Blaze to find who ordered it, kills the culprit and stays there. Eimi breaks her own seal; deploying her too often causes the bad end.
- **C.** All the conflicts resolve quickly, and Nessiah panics and becomes the villain, with a breakdown.

**Later names.** Leon (19, ex-Imperial Guard, parents executed as scapegoats), Baldus, Pandra (rebel leader), Eudy (cannon girl), Zilva (assassin), Mizer (barefoot bounty hunter), Inzaghi, Russell, Baretreenu (Garlot's mother? seals blood), Alanjame, King Ordene, Yggdra as a child.

Nessiah is a fallen angel "Aries", a secret never stated in this game: keep his hints vague. Don't use Gulcasa, Emilia for Garlot, Eimi before the script does.
